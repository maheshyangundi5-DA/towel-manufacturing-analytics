## 📌 Project Overview

Towel Manufacturing Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing business operations and performance across a towel manufacturing organization.

The project integrates data preparation, SQL querying, Python analysis, and Power BI visualization to transform raw business data into meaningful insights.

It covers multiple business functions, including sales, production, employee performance, machinery, customers, products, suppliers, warehouses, inventory, quality, maintenance, dispatch, procurement, and finance.

The primary objective is to provide management with a comprehensive view of manufacturing operations through data-driven reporting and interactive dashboards.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales revenue and profitability.
- Monitor production quantity, time, and manufacturing costs.
- Evaluate employee and machine performance.
- Analyze machine capacity and utilization.
- Understand customer and product performance.
- Evaluate supplier contributions and purchasing activities.
- Monitor warehouse capacity and inventory value.
- Analyze quality performance, defects, and rejections.
- Evaluate maintenance activities and costs.
- Monitor dispatch, delivery, and shipping performance.
- Analyze purchase versus consumption.
- Provide financial and executive-level reporting.
